2. Fonts, images and sound
--------------------------

- Fonts: none are shipped. The interface uses the visitor's system fonts
  (system-ui and platform defaults). In-game lettering is a pixel font
  defined in GALAXIA's own source code.
- Images: no third-party images, photographs or telescope imagery are
  shipped. Sprites, icons and backdrops are drawn by GALAXIA's own code.
- Sound: no recordings or music files are shipped. Every sound is
  synthesised in the browser from short recipes in GALAXIA's own code.

-----------------------------------

short fact lines are written in GALAXIA's own words from public sources.
No source text or imagery is reproduced. Credit is given to:
